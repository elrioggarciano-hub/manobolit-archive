1.11 RECONCILING FRAMEWORK-ONLY: STEP 5 CHECK 4 (13/22) VS STEP 5 DIAG (12/22)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Root-Cause Analysis of the Entry 19 ("Damgo") Discrepancy:
1. In engine.ts previously: rawText was processed by baseNormalize(parts.join(" ")) BEFORE cleanClassificationText.
   baseNormalize (normalizeText) stripped punctuation including ":" into spaces, converting:
   "Answer: Pag mata nag damgo ra ka" -> "answer pag mata nag damgo ra ka".
2. stripRiddleAnswer regex (\b(?:Answer|Tubag)\s*:[^\n\r]*) required a colon (:). Because the colon was already
   stripped by baseNormalize, the answer was NOT stripped. The word "damgo" remained in the classified text.
3. G005 (RIDDLE) matched keyword "damgo" (confidence 0.37, baseConfidence 0.92), while G003 (FOLKTALE) matched
   keyword "tiger" (confidence 0.36, baseConfidence 0.90). RIDDLE won by 0.01 margin (13/22 in Check 4).
4. In contrast, step5-diag.ts passed raw un-normalized text directly into cleanClassificationText, where the colon
   was still present, successfully stripping the answer and "damgo". Only "tiger" matched, so FOLKTALE won (12/22).
5. Resolution: engine.ts now applies cleanClassificationText BEFORE baseNormalize. Both code paths are now identical.
