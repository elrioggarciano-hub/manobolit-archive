## 6. UNCERTAINTIES & KNOWN ARCHITECTURAL LIMITS

1. Small Sample Size (N=22): The archive dataset comprises 22 authenticated records (8 riddles, 5 proverbs, 4 folktales, 5 songs). Statistical confidence intervals around Macro-F1 (0.446) remain wide. Metrics must be interpreted as exploratory empirical benchmarks rather than asymptotic bounds.
2. Zero-Shot Class Sparsity: Standalone Naive Bayes and TF-IDF VSM models trained on 21 short documents suffer from extreme out-of-vocabulary sparsity on held-out entries; individual model scores frequently fall below the 0.35 threshold and abstain to UNCLASSIFIED.
3. Syntax Insensitivity (Shuffled Riddle Anomaly): Unigram bag-of-words and independent lexical pattern matchers are permutation-invariant across words. They cannot detect ungrammatical scrambled token sequences.
4. Inactive Genre Pruning: MYTH, LEGEND, EPIC, and CHANT remain inactive in live predictions due to the absence of authenticated archive records in the current field collection.
