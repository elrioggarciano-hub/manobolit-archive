PART 3: FUZZY BONUS ARCHITECTURAL DECISION REPORT
=========================================================================================
- Evaluation: The previous implementation added an arbitrary scalar bonus (+0.15) to every
  genre whenever a generic 'GENRE' variant (e.g. 'tudtul') matched anywhere in the text.
- Architectural Choice: REMOVED additive fuzzy bonus from the genre ensemble combination.
- Academic Defense Rationale: Additive scalar bonuses disrupt the mathematical calibration
  of normalized probability distributions (sum = 1.0). In low-resource text classification,
  dialectal variations are already captured robustly by the character 3-5-gram subword model.
- Diagnostic Retention: Fuzzy orthographic matches remain active in algorithmicBreakdown.fuzzyMatches
  for transparency and cultural-linguistic inspection in the user interface.
